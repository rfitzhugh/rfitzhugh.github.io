I'm an engineering leader currently building something new. For two decades I’ve worked at the intersection of developer platforms, engineering productivity, and cloud infrastructure. 

At [Atlassian](https://www.atlassian.com) I helped shape developer infrastructure across R&D, with a focus on AI-native development. During that time, I led productivity work that cut pull request cycle time by 60% and improved CI build speed by 25%. Earlier at [Rubrik](https://www.rubrik.com/), I built the developer relations function from the ground up and developed ecosystem integrations and developer tooling, extending the platform through partnerships with HashiCorp (Terraform), Red Hat (Ansible), and VMware. 

Before that, I founded and ran Technicloud, a boutique consulting firm helping enterprises through the cloud-native transition. I have an M.A. in International Business Policy from Georgetown University and a B.A. in International Relations from Penn State. Further back, I served in the Marine Corps.
